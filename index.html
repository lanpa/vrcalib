How to calibrate
  1. Scan the phone QR code (right) with the phone. Enter its PPI if asked and tap Start, then put the phone in the viewer.
  2. Measure with a ruler and enter screen-to-lens, inter-lens and tray-to-lens distances.
  3. Fine-tune inter-lens and tray-to-lens until each red dot sits in the middle of its lens and the two images fuse.
  4. Tune k1, then k2, until grid lines are straight right to the edges. Lines bowing outward (pincushion): raise k1. Bowing inward (barrel): lower it. k2 mostly affects the outer edge.
  5. Reduce field-of-view angles only to trim leftover red at the edges.
  6. Scan the export QR code with a Cardboard app.

Tab / Shift+Tab move between fields. ↑ / ↓ change a number by 0.5 mm, 0.01 (k) or 1°; hold Shift for ×10.
